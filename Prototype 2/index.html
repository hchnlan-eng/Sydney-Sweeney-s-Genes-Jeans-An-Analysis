Voyeurism

Technique 01

Visual gaze and camera movement

Slow upward tracking from crotch to direct eye contact shifts Kress & van Leeuwen’s (2006) “offer” gaze into a “demand” gaze, operationalising Laura Mulvey’s (1975) “to-be-looked-at-ness”, Christian Metz’s (1982) “unauthorised keyhole scopophilia”, and Guy Cook’s (2001) erotic mood as dressing turns into consumable undressing.

Kress & van Leeuwen, 2006; Mulvey, 1975; Metz, 1982; Cook, 2001

Technique 02

Sound and simulated proximity

Slow cadence, breathy inhalation, raspy vocal fry, and soft sighs compensate for physical absence, simulating intimate proximity and coding femininity as submissive to patriarchal fantasy.

Metz, 1982

Technique 03

Racial symbol and exnomination

Minimal lighting, natural makeup, and classic blue denim frame simplicity as purity, asserting whiteness as the neutral, unmarked default standard via exnomination.

Exnomination · Default whiteness

Somatic hierarchy

01 Grammatical causation

Hallidayan transitivity and erased agency

In “Genes are passed down from parents to offspring, often determining traits like hair color, personality, and even eye color”, Halliday’s (1994) transitivity maps Actor (“Genes”) → Material process (“determining”) → Goal (“traits”), erasing casting directors, stylists, lighting technicians, and editors so Eurocentric beauty reads as an automatic law of nature.

Halliday, 1994

02 Adjectival fusion and reification

Nordic traits as commodity sign

Cook’s (2001) adjectival fusion links “eye color” to “My jeans are blue.”, while Goldman’s (1992) reification packages blonde hair, blue eyes, a slim waist, and a large bust as an aspirational commodity sign—pressuring women outside that template to internalise structural racial exclusion as personal biological failure.

Cook, 2001; Goldman, 1992

Post-feminist alibis

Alibi 01

Sexual subjectification

Unlike Brooke Shields’ full-body stage distance, Sweeney’s spatial proximity and direct eye contact frame her as an active, self-directing agent—packaging voluntary self-fetishisation as autonomous “midriff” agency (“feminism taken into account”, “reprivatisation”, “commodity feminism”) while casting critics as the “feminist thought police”.

Gill, 2007, 2008; McRobbie, 2004; McNay, 1992; Goldman, 1992

Alibi 02

Moral currency

Perceived gender progression is traded to offset racial regression: white “power femininity” erases racial difference and reserves “knowing” post-feminist sexual agency exclusively for white women, creating a moral buffer that insulates Nordic genetic hierarchy from scrutiny.

Lazar, 2006; Gill, 2008

Alibi 03

Institutional insulation

On Instagram, disjunctive fragments (“Her jeans. Her story.”) and “Great jeans look good on everyone.” deploy colourblind non-performativity as a PR shield; on podcasts, “double entanglement” and Strategy 4 reversal reframe anti-racist critics as “woke” bullies attacking a “normal” all-American woman.

Delin, 2000; Bonilla-Silva, 2003; Ahmed, 2006; McRobbie, 2004; van Dijk, 1992
